      WELCOME TO THE LOCKER ROOM

      Make the connection.

      In Daily and Shortest Chain, each player you add counts as one shot; an automatic destination link takes no extra shot. Snake counts every teammate link as a shot. Players must have recorded an NHL appearance for the same team in the same season, including playoffs. They do not need to have played in the same game; traded teammates can count even if their roster dates did not overlap.

      Shortest Chain

      Daily games and Shortest Chain aim for the fewest shots. Adding a player who connects to the destination finishes automatically in one shot, including the destination link. Each player can be used once. Free-play random games choose a verified minimum of 1, 2, 3, or 4 shots. Some era and rule combinations support fewer lengths.

      Position challenges: Blue Line allows defensemen as intermediate players; Between the Pipes allows goalies; Forward Lines allows forwards. Open Roster allows anyone. The starting and destination players are unrestricted. Minimum shots and all revealed answers follow the position rule. Daily games use Open Roster.

      Snake: Open Ice

      Make the longest chain across any years in your selected decades. Change teams on every shot. Each team can appear at most twice across the entire chain, in different seasons. Each season can be used at most three times total. Use each player and each team-season once. A dead end ends the round, with no undo; restart to try again.

      Snake: Career Run

      Your first connection must use the starting player’s rookie season. Every next connection uses a strictly later season; skipping years is allowed. Finish with the destination in their final recorded season. Active players use their latest season in this dataset. Score counts seasons linked, with years covered shown alongside it. Use each player once.

      Risky moves are allowed. If no chronological finish remains, the run ends. There is no undo in Career Run. Restart the same matchup to try another route. Your selected decades must include both endpoint seasons.

      Snake: Road Trip

      Visit the most teams. Years may move in either direction. Each player and each team can appear only once, even in a different season. Your score is teams visited.

      Road Trip protects a route to the destination where one can be verified. Snake does not automatically finish when a player connects to the destination—choose when to take that final shot. The answer button searches for a longest valid route; it says “longest route found” unless a maximum has been proven.

      Daily stars

      Earn 3 stars by matching the shortest route shown above the players. One extra shot earns 2 stars; two or more extra shots earn 1 star. Browsing and hints do not reduce your stars. Revealing the answer earns no stars.

      Exploring and hints

      Exploring is always available. You can browse seasons to find teammates, or type a player’s name directly into the search box. Tap a headshot or expand Teams & seasons under a player. Tap a season to browse that team’s roster. Browsing takes no shots. Add to chain takes one shot and uses the season you explored. Used players are marked. Team, Roster, and Player hints follow your game’s rules and count toward hints used.

      In Shortest Chain, decade settings select starting and destination players only; connections, hints, answers, and roster exploration can use all years. Snake modes restrict connection seasons to the selected decades. Career Run also requires later seasons. Daily games include all decades.

      Daily progress and streaks are saved on this device. Revealing the daily answer ends the attempt and does not earn a streak. Free-play share links preserve your players, decades, Snake style or position rule. After Shortest Chain games, browse every shortest player chain; team-season variants of the same player chain count as one answer.
