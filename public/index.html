Which decades count?

Only connections from seasons starting in your selected decades count. Choose one or combine several.

Free-play options

Longest Chain: use as many unique players as you can. Every link needs a new team-season, and seasons must stay the same or move forward. Random games favor wide career spans and players with many connections. All decades are selected when you switch to this style; you can narrow them. Reaching the destination ends the game.

Difficulty controls random starting and destination players. All players in your selected decades can be used as connections. Easy uses a curated star list, not season rankings; Medium uses career regular-season games.

Random matchup

Each new game randomly chooses a minimum of 2, 3, or 4 shots. Available lengths depend on your selected decades and difficulty.

    WELCOME TO THE LOCKER ROOM

    Make the connection.

    Build a chain from the starting player to the destination. Each pair must have recorded an NHL appearance for the same team in the same season, including playoffs.

    They don’t need to have played in the same game. A trade can count even if their dates on the team didn’t overlap.

    Search a name to add a player. Each valid link shows its team and season. Once you’re one link away, connect to the destination.

    Minimum shots is the fewest possible connections. Each valid connection counts as one shot. In Shortest Chain, adding a player who connects to the destination automatically completes that final connection. Longest Chain requires you to finish explicitly. Each player and team-season can appear once, and seasons cannot go backward. The same team can be used again in a later season; changing teams in the same season is allowed. Your next link must leave a legal forward route to the destination. There’s no move limit—try to match the minimum. Undo is allowed. Revealing the route ends the daily attempt and won’t earn a streak.

    Team hints show the team names for your next link on a shortest route, without naming the player. Hints are counted in your shared result.

    Explore mode in daily games and free play lets you tap seasons to browse team rosters and player careers. Browsing takes no shots; Add to chain takes one shot. Used players are marked. Daily games include all seasons; free play uses your selected decades. Roster and Player hints help you finish and count toward hints used.

    Decade settings apply to free play. They restrict the seasons used for every link, not just the players’ career years. The daily challenge uses all decades so everyone gets the same puzzle.

    After finishing, see your own route explained with team-season evidence and browse every shortest player chain. Alternative answers are loaded 20 at a time. Different shared seasons for the same chain do not count as different answers.

    Daily progress and streaks are saved on this device. Free play lets you choose two players or generate a random matchup with a minimum of 2, 3, or 4 shots from selected decades. Choose Shortest Chain for the fewest shots or Longest Chain for as many unique players as you can. Reaching the destination ends your game. Team histories expand beneath each player in your chain; bold teams and seasons count in selected decades. Create a share link to challenge someone with the same players and decade rules. Historical source caveats are documented in the repository.
