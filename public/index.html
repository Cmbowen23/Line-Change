    WELCOME TO THE LOCKER ROOM

    Make the connection.

    Build a chain from the starting player to the destination. Each pair must have recorded an NHL appearance for the same team in the same season, including playoffs.

    They don’t need to have played in the same game. A trade can count even if their dates on the team didn’t overlap.

    Search a name to add a player. Each valid link shows its team and season. Once you’re one link away, connect to the destination.

    Minimum shots is the fewest possible connections. Each valid connection counts as one shot. In Shortest Chain, adding a player who connects to the destination automatically completes that final connection. Longest Chain requires you to finish explicitly. There’s no move limit—try to match the minimum. Undo is allowed. Revealing the route ends the daily attempt and won’t earn a streak.

    Team hints show the team names for your next link on a shortest route, without naming the player. Hints are counted in your shared result.

    Explore mode in free play lets you tap seasons to browse team rosters and player careers. Browsing takes no shots; Add to chain takes one shot. Used players are marked. Only seasons in your selected decades are clickable. Roster and Player hints help you finish and count toward hints used.

    Decade settings apply to free play. They restrict the seasons used for every link, not just the players’ career years. The daily challenge uses all decades so everyone gets the same puzzle.

    After finishing, see your own route explained with team-season evidence and browse every shortest player chain. Alternative answers are loaded 20 at a time. Different shared seasons for the same chain do not count as different answers.

    Daily progress and streaks are saved on this device. Free play lets you choose two players or generate a random matchup from selected decades. Choose Shortest Chain for the fewest shots or Longest Chain for as many unique players as you can. Reaching the destination ends your game. Team histories expand beneath each player in your chain; bold teams and seasons count in selected decades. Create a share link to challenge someone with the same players and decade rules. Historical source caveats are documented in the repository.
